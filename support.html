How to log

Type an abbreviation and a time on one line, then press Return.

  • lis 30 — 30 minutes of Listening, just finished
  • lis 1005-1010 or lis 10am-11am — a time range
  • lis 1.5h or lis 1,5h — 90 minutes
  • lis — start or stop a timer
  • lis 30 / voc 15 — log two at once

Tap ? above the input field for the full guide.

FAQ

Something I typed was saved as “Review”.

Hourstride never rejects what you type. Anything it can’t read is saved and marked “Review” so you can fix it later in History.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Settings › Export Backup (JSON) and then Restore from Backup on the new device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries or activities in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Study late at night?

In Settings, set “Day Starts At” to something like 4 AM so that after-midnight sessions count toward the same day.
