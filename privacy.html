Privacy Policy

Hourstride · Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Hourstride keeps everything on your iPhone. We do not collect, send, or sell any of your data.

Data you enter

Your study entries, activities, groups, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Hourstride has no account, no server, and does not connect to the internet.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Hourstride contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Exports

When you export your data (CSV or JSON), the file goes only where you choose to send or save it through the iOS share sheet.

Backups

Your data may be included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backup, according to your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple, not by us.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you log entries with Siri or the Shortcuts app, Apple processes your request under Apple’s privacy policy. Hourstride receives only the text of the entry and stores it on your device.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

Hourstride collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it in the app and on this page, along with the date above.

Questions? See the support page.
